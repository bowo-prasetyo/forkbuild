# Now, Next, Later

What ForkBuild works on, in order. At most three items in each list.
Nothing new starts while an item in **Now** is unfinished, unless the owner
moves it ([Pillars.md](Pillars.md), "The filter"). When an item is done,
it leaves this page, its roadmap entry says what was built, and the next
one moves up. The [roadmap](Roadmap.md) stays the record of what shipped
and why.

Set on 2026-10-22, from
[the creative-direction review of that day](reviews/CreativeDirection-2026-10-22.md)
and the owner's four decisions on it.

## Now: 1.6, "First publish"

The launch week's counts showed 24 daily visits, 10 Editor opens, 3 first
edits and no build published. Everything here aims at that drop
(`/e/editor-edit` → `/e/publish-*`, and the median of
`/e/publish-placed-…`).

1. **Publish in one sheet.** A suggested name, with **Others may remix
   it** and **Everyone can find it** already on and changeable in place,
   and one **Publish** button. No dead end; publishing unsigned moves under
   Advanced (Pillars, "Infrastructure, kept out of sight").
2. **A first job worth doing.** The starter job becomes a short chain
   (roof → chimney → a path to the door → a fence or a garden), ending
   near ten placed bricks. One tutor at a time on every screen: while a job
   is open, the first-build guide folds to its pill and no toast repeats
   it. The guide's third step becomes **Add something of your own**.
3. **A first walk that looks like the game.** The first World View visit of
   a page opens in fair daylight; every build and the plaza stand in a
   clearing; "Walk inside it" arrives with nothing between the camera and
   the door (Pillars, "The World is a diorama, not a simulation").

Riding along with these: the launch kit's known issues corrected (Nostr
needs no extension now), the four `forkbuild-document-*.json` example
builds moved out of the repository root, **Edit a Copy** hidden on your own
build, and the toolbar's **Publish** in the one meadow green.

## Next

1. **A playtest, written down.** Five people who play building games, given
   only the Home link, two of them on a phone; every place they stall, with
   a time, in `docs/playtests/`. Its top three fixes join **Now**.
2. **One launch channel for builders.** A cozy-game or building-game
   community, with a 30-second clip (*Try it now → roof → Walk inside*).
3. **A week of counts read** with `node scripts/launch-scorecard.mjs`. The
   biggest drop in FIRST STEPS chooses what comes after.

## Later

1. **The diorama pass.** The World's trees and water brought to the bricks'
   scale and level of detail, simple trees by default (Pillars, "The World
   is a diorama, not a simulation").
2. **Seasons on the challenge week** (Pillars, "One clock that matters: the
   week").
3. **Pillars.md back to one page:** the fantasy, the pillars, the filter,
   Words, Tone, Look and what we're not making; the networks, the side
   systems and "Where this came from" moved to a page of decisions.

## Not now

More weather, water, sky or creature work; more voice building; more
look-alike work; new vehicles or traversal; new networks; new languages.
Each needs the owner's yes and a reason in the counts (Pillars, "The
filter", question 4).
