# Press kit

## One line

> ForkBuild: build in 3D in your browser, remix anything, own your work.

## One paragraph

> ForkBuild is a free, open-source 3D building app that runs in the browser.
> People snap bricks into houses, bridges and whole villages, fork any
> published build into a copy of their own that remembers where it came
> from, and share builds as links that carry the build itself. There are no
> accounts: identities are key pairs on the device, builds are signed, and
> people connect to each other directly.

## Facts

- **Site:** <https://bowo-prasetyo.github.io/forkbuild/>
- **Source:** <https://github.com/bowo-prasetyo/forkbuild>, Mozilla Public
  License 2.0
- **itch.io:** <https://forkbuild.itch.io/forkbuild>
- **Runs in:** any current browser, desktop or phone; installable as an app,
  and works offline
- **Languages:** English, German, Spanish, French, Bahasa Indonesia,
  Japanese, Korean, Brazilian Portuguese
- **Made with:** JavaScript modules, Vue 3, Three.js, WebRTC; optional
  publishing to Nostr, Arweave, IPFS, Steem and Blurt
- **Privacy:** [docs/Privacy.md](../Privacy.md) lists every server it
  contacts
