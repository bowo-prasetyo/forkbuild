# Product Hunt

Product Hunt days run from 12:01 am US Pacific; launch then, and be there all
day. A product needs a maker account, a name, a tagline, a description,
gallery images, and a first comment.

**Link:** <https://bowo-prasetyo.github.io/forkbuild/?ref=producthunt>

## Name

ForkBuild

## Tagline (60 characters at most)

> Build in 3D in your browser. Remix anything. Own your work.

## Description (260 characters at most)

> ForkBuild is a 3D building app in the browser. Snap bricks into houses,
> bridges and villages, fork any published build into your own copy, and
> share it with a link that carries the build itself. No account, no
> download; your work stays on your device.

## Topics

Design Tools, Games, Open Source, Web App, 3D.

## First comment (as the maker)

> Hey Product Hunt! I built ForkBuild because building things in 3D should
> be as easy to share and remix as code. Open it, pick a ready-made castle or
> house, change it, and share a link: whoever opens it sees your build in 3D
> and can make their own copy in one click, no account needed.
>
> This week's build challenge is <theme>: join from the home page and post
> your link here, I'd love to see them.
>
> It's free and open source. Everything you make stays in your browser unless
> you choose to publish it.
