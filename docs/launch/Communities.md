# Nostr, Steem and Blurt

ForkBuild already announces builds on these networks, so their users can
both try it and find each other's builds in it. Post as yourself, from the
account you publish builds with.

## Nostr

A note with the `#forkbuild` and the week's challenge hashtag, a picture
(**Save picture** on a published build) and a shared link to that build,
then the site:

> Made this <theme> in ForkBuild, a 3D building app that announces builds on
> Nostr: anyone can open it in 3D and fork it from the link, no account.
> This week's build challenge is <theme>; your entry shows up on everyone's
> challenge page once you distribute it to Nostr.
>
> <shared link to the build>
> https://bowo-prasetyo.github.io/forkbuild/?ref=nostr
>
> #forkbuild #<week's tag> #3d #buildinpublic

Distributing a build to Nostr needs a NIP-07 signer extension (nos2x, Alby
and others); say so to anyone who asks why their entry doesn't show.

## Steem and Blurt

A post in the communities that take creative and open-source work, with
pictures, a shared link and the site (`?ref=steem` or `?ref=blurt`). Blurt
posts that ForkBuild makes itself already list a build's tags, so a
challenge entry distributed to Blurt carries the week's tag there too, and
upvotes earn its maker BLURT.

Ask in each community whether a weekly ForkBuild challenge post would be
welcome before making it a habit.
